I am a CS PhD student at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by <a href="https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~sha9/" target="_blank">Sehoon Ha</a> and <a href="https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~danfei/" target="_blank">Danfei Xu</a>. My research focuses on leveraging egocentric human data for robot mobile manipulation. I also received my MS in Computer Science (thesis track) from Georgia Tech in 2026.

Before coming to Georgia Tech, I received a BSc in Computer Science with First-Class Honours from the University of Edinburgh. As an undergraduate researcher at <a href="https://maps-lab.github.io/" target="_blank">MAPS Lab</a>, supervised by <a href="https://christopherlu.github.io/" target="_blank">Prof. Chris Lu</a>, I built <a href="https://thermohands.github.io/" target="_blank">ThermoHands</a>, the first multispectral, multiview benchmark for egocentric hand pose estimation, and developed a transformer-based hand pose estimator for egocentric thermal images.
